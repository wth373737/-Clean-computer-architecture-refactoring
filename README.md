# -Clean-computer-architecture-refactoring
现代计算机体系充满了各种拍脑袋的词汇和莫名其妙的翻译，这已经极大阻碍了计算机领域的发展和未来，毫无意义地增加了学习难度。现在想要改变问题，代价极大。人人都在考虑自身的利益和群体的意志而放弃改变。但是未来想要改变，代价只会更大。我无法忍受现在的人继续损害未来，我要尝试创造一个“平行世界”。种树不是为了自己乘凉，而是因为相信总会有人需要树荫，我淋过的雨就不应该留给后来者重蹈覆辙。
Modern computer systems are riddled with spurious, poorly chosen terms and nonsensical translations, severely hindering the development and future of the computer field and needlessly increasing the difficulty of learning. Changing the problem now comes at a tremendous cost. Everyone prioritizes their own interests and the will of the group, thus abandoning change. But changing the future will only come at a higher cost. I cannot tolerate people continuing to harm the future; I want to try to create a "parallel world." Planting trees isn't for my own shade, but because I believe someone will always need it, and the rain I've been through shouldn't be left for future generations to repeat.
